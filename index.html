About SADIWA

SADIWA is a comprehensive system designed to document, monitor, and visualize earthquake-affected heritage churches in Bohol, Philippines. It enables community participation in preserving our cultural heritage.

🗺️

Interactive Map

Visualize all heritage churches in Bohol with risk-coded markers on an interactive map powered by Leaflet.js.

📝

Damage Reporting

Community contributors can submit detailed damage reports with photos and risk assessments.

📊

Risk Analysis

View comprehensive reports and statistics on church conditions and risk levels across the province.

User Roles

Admin
  • Manage church records
  • Approve/reject reports
  • Full system access
Contributor
  • View map and risk analysis
  • Submit damage reports
  • Upload photos
Viewer
  • View map and risk analysis
  • View churches
  • View reports
